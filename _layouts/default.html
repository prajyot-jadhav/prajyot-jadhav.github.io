Prajyot Jadhav

I’m currently pursuing my Master’s in Robotics (MSR) at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Frederike Dümbgen in the TRIO Lab. I’m working on sample-efficient policy learning by using foundation models to guide search in low-dimensional subspaces of deep RL policies.

Before CMU, I was a Research Engineer in Robotics at the AKS Lab of the University of Tartu, Estonia, advised by Prof. Arun Kumar Singh. Previously, I also worked with JdeRobot through Google Summer of Code, collaborating with Prof. José María Cañas Plaza and Prof. Pedro Arias Pérez on the open-source software Robotics Academy.

I hold a Bachelor’s in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), India. During my time there, I was part of IvLabs, the AI and Robotics lab of VNIT, where I developed many of my research interests under the guidance of Prof. Shital Chiddarwar. During the summer after my junior year, I worked on adaptive control strategies for quadrotors under the supervision of Prof. Poramate Manoonpong.
